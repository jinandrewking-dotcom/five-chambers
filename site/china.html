Clay, memory & momentum

February 2026 · China

Jingdezhen → Changsha: world treasure to influential new city.

JingdezhenChangshaporcelain & tea

The route

Jingdezhen has been firing porcelain for more than a thousand years, and it is still a working town rather than a museum: studios, kilns and clay dust on the backstreets. For a long time it was a name everyone knew and few visited; now a younger crowd is rediscovering it, coming to throw pots rather than just buy them.

Changsha is the other end of the story: a city that turned tea drinks, late-night food streets and shopping-mall nostalgia into national brands. As someone who reads consumer data for a living, that's what I came to watch: how a young crowd spends, queues and photographs. In between, a proper tea tasting: fresh green leaves, several cups, no hurry.
