What it means on a sales floor

  • Registrations lag, finance leads. VFACTS tells you what already happened. The forward-looking signals are finance applications and used-EV auction volumes. That's where a quarter gets won or lost.
  • The objection script is changing. With battery-electric cars near a quarter of sales, "where do I charge?" gives way to questions about running costs, battery health and resale. The floor needs answers on future value, not maps of chargers.
  • Mix moved before volume did. Electrified drivetrains passed half of August sales (see the stat cards). The opportunity is substitution within the market, not a bigger market.
  • State policy is a real variable. The spread between the highest and lowest state isn't consumer taste. It comes from rebate design, fleet procurement and the charging rollout.
