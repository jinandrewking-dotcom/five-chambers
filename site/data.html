What it means on a sales floor

  • Registrations lag, finance leads. VFACTS tells you what already happened. The forward-looking signals are finance applications and used-EV auction volumes — that is where a quarter gets won or lost.
  • Charging anxiety flipped sides. The NRMA's 42,472-member survey found EV owners are now less worried about finding a charger than petrol owners are about fuel prices. That inverts the objection script the floor has used for years.
  • Mix moved before volume did. Electrified drivetrains reached 53.9% of August sales while total market growth ran at roughly 5% year-on-year. The opportunity is substitution, not market expansion.
  • State policy is a real variable. A 19-point spread between the ACT and the Northern Territory is not consumer taste — it is rebate design, fleet procurement and charging rollout.
