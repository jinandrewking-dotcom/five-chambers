Rainbows in the Dharma hall.

Compassion without rights is just a slogan. Showing up as an ally at work and in the community — because equity work doesn't stop at the temple gate.

allyshipinclusive workplacespride

The story so far

Every March I march in Sydney's Mardi Gras with the community, alongside USYD, ANTRA and ACON. The rest of the year it's smaller and steadier: a rainbow archery session, turning up for the queer group at uni, and pushing for “inclusive workplace” to mean something on an ordinary Tuesday, not just in Mardi Gras season.

For me this isn't separate from practice. Compassion that stops at the edge of who's comfortable isn't compassion, and the Dharma hall is a better place when everyone in it can be fully themselves.
