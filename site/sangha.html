The story so far

I practise Humanistic Buddhism (人间佛教) in the Fo Guang Shan tradition founded by Venerable Master Hsing Yun: the idea that practice belongs in ordinary life, at work, at the table and in traffic, not only on retreat. Time at Fo Guang Shan's Tsung Lin University in Taiwan showed me what that looks like when study and daily routine are the same thing.

These days my reading moves between the Āgamas, the early discourses, and papers on AI and consciousness. The overlap is closer than it sounds: both ask what a mind is made of, and whether anything in it is as solid as it feels. Meditation is where I check my answers.
