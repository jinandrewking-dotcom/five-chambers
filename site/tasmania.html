Blood, sweat & the highway

April 2026 · Tasmania

Launceston → Hobart → Launceston through National Highway 1.

Highway 1Tasmaniaself-drive

The route

Highway 1 runs down the spine of the island, from Launceston on the Tamar to Hobart on the Derwent, and I drove it there and back. The Midlands in between are quiet sheep-and-hay country, but almost everything built along the way (the bridges, the sandstone towns, much of the road itself) was built by convict labour. Ross Bridge, finished in 1836, still carries traffic.

That's what “blood and sweat” means in the road log: most of the colony's first arrivals didn't choose to come, and they came to land that had been palawa country for tens of thousands of years. One end of the drive gave me sandstone warehouses and a paper map read on the footpath; the other gave me vineyards under bird netting at dusk.
