Tea & the Way茶禅行者

One pot, one mind.

The gongfu table is my second meditation cushion. Brewing slowly, sipping slowly, and believing 禅茶一味 — the Way and the tea are one single flavour.

gongfu cha禅茶一味stillness

The story so far

Gongfu cha (功夫茶) is a small, exact ritual: warm the pot, rinse the leaves, then short infusions, each one tasted for how it has changed since the last. It rewards attention and punishes hurry, which is why it works as my second meditation cushion.

禅茶一味, Chan and tea as one flavour, is the idea I keep coming back to. The same leaves are bright in the third cup and quiet by the eighth; nothing stays, and the noticing is the practice. A teahouse in China or a simple set at my own table, it's the pace that matters more than the tea.
