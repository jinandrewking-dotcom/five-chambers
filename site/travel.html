One pedal, one throttle, many stories.

New Zealand's South Island and Australia's coastlines, driven alone. A full tank, a half-baked plan, and my own company for company.

NZ South Islandcoastal AustraliaTasman seaself-drive

The story so far

I drive alone on purpose. A long road with nobody to talk to is the closest thing I have to walking meditation: the mind wanders, comes back, wanders again, and the kilometres keep count.

So far the long ones have been Tasmania's convict-built Highway 1, Jingdezhen to Changsha, and New Zealand's South Island, Queenstown to the lakes. Each has its own page with a route map and photos, and the full list lives in the road log.
